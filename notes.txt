###  Remote access  ###

Add-on: Tailscale/WireGuard

Regardless of how you connect, take these basic steps once your system is running:
    - Turn on Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Enable this in your Home Assistant user profile immediately so a stolen password cannot grant access to your home.
    - Avoid basic port forwarding: Never simply open port 8123 on your router to the internet without encryption (HTTPS) and a reverse proxy, as automated bots constantly scan for exposed smart home hubs.
    - Keep backups off-device: Set up automated backups to Google Drive or a local NAS so you can recover quickly if the SSD ever fails.
